Privacy Policy

Smart Genie ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized usage statistics to improve app features.
  • AI Interactions: Text and images sent to AI features are processed by third-party AI providers (Google Gemini). We do not permanently store your AI conversation content.
  • Purchase Information: Google Play handles all payment processing. We store only the purchase status.
  • Advertising Data: For non-premium users, Start.io and Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and device information to serve relevant ads.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our AI-powered utility features
  • To manage your account and premium subscription status
  • To display relevant advertisements (non-premium users only)
  • To analyze app usage patterns for improvement

Advertising

We use Start.io (primary) and Google AdMob (secondary) to display advertisements in the free version of the App.

  • Start.io: Start.io collects device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to provide relevant ads. Start.io Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: AdMob collects device identifiers, location data, and usage data to provide personalized ads. Google Advertising Policy
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID).
  • Premium Users: Premium subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience — no data is shared with ad networks.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS) with encryption in transit and at rest. Firebase Authentication handles secure sign-in.

Third-Party Services

  • Start.io: Primary ad network — Privacy Policy | Terms of Use
  • Google AdMob: Secondary ad network — Advertising Policy
  • Google Firebase: Authentication and analytics
  • Google Gemini AI: AI processing
  • Supabase: Database and backend services
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases

Your Rights

  • Data Access: View your data within app settings.
  • Data Deletion: Delete your account and all data from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Smart Genie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary. You can request account deletion at any time.
